Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Visit the Help Centre.

How do I apply for a job?

Sign up as a candidate, open a job and choose Apply. Upload your CV with each application, then follow its status — submitted, reviewed, interviewing or accepted — from your dashboard.

Can I save jobs and apply later?

Yes. Select Save on any job and it appears under Saved jobs in your dashboard.

How do I post a job?

Sign up as a recruiter and choose Post a job. Add the title, company, location and description; pick a company you've used before or add a new one with its logo.

Can employers search candidates' CVs?

Yes. Recruiters can search stored CVs by their requirements and shortlist candidates. Access depends on your plan — see pricing for employers.

How do I log in?

Use your email address and password, or continue with your Google account.
